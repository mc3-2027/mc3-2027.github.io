About the conference

Advancing mineral carbonation science for cement and concrete

3MC3 will bring together researchers, engineers, and industry professionals working at the intersection of CO2 mineralization, cement chemistry, concrete technology, SCMs, and carbon capture and utilization.

3MC3 is the third conference in the Mineral Carbonation for Cement and Concrete series. The 1st edition took place at RWTH Aachen University, Germany, in 2024, and the 2nd edition took place at Tokyo University in Japan in 2026. The Bilbao meeting continues the series with a strong emphasis on scientific depth, international participation, and discussion across laboratory, pilot, and industrial scales.

The conference will also include a special session linked to the EU MSCA Doctoral Network CONTRABASS, creating dedicated space for doctoral research, training-oriented exchange, and discussion on carbonation of calcium silicates and aluminates in low-carbon cement systems.

Scientific scope

Conference topics

01

Carbonation mechanisms

Reaction pathways, kinetics, thermodynamics, moisture effects, and phase evolution.

02

CO2 mineralization routes

Capture, utilization, and mineral binding strategies for cement-based systems.

03

Secondary resources and SCMs

Valorization of slags, ashes, recycled fines, and other alkaline residues.

04

Low-carbon binders

Alternative cementitious binders, blended systems, and carbonation-enabled formulations.

05

Testing, modeling, and characterization

Advanced experimental methods, thermodynamic assessment, and microstructural analysis.

06

Upscaling and concrete performance

Industrial implementation, durability, mechanical behavior, and lifecycle assessment.

Venue

Bilbao, Spain

Bilbao offers a distinctive setting for 3MC3, combining industrial heritage, materials innovation, and internationally recognized architecture along the Nervion estuary, with strong relevance for sustainable construction and industrial decarbonization research.

3MC3 will take place at Bizkaia Aretoa, the University of the Basque Country's conference venue in Bilbao. Venue information and travel guidance are available on a dedicated page.
