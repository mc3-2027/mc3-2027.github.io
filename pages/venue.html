Conference venue

Bizkaia Aretoa

3MC3 will be hosted at Bizkaia Aretoa, the University of the Basque Country's conference venue in Bilbao. The venue brings the meeting into the centre of the city and provides a dedicated setting for the scientific programme and discussions.

Explore the region

Bilbao and Bizkaia

Bilbao combines industrial heritage, contemporary architecture, culture, and a growing materials-innovation ecosystem. The wider Bizkaia region offers coastal landscapes, food, museums, and easy opportunities to extend a conference visit.
