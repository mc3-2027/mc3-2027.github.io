Conference venue

Bizkaia Aretoa

3MC3 will be hosted at Bizkaia Aretoa, the University of the Basque Country's conference venue in Bilbao. The venue brings the meeting into the centre of the city and provides a dedicated setting for the scientific programme and discussions.

Getting to Bilbao

Travel information

By plane and public transport

Bilbao Airport (BIO) offers direct connections with major Spanish and European cities. From the airport, the Bizkaibus A3247 service provides a direct link to central Bilbao throughout the year, stopping at Alameda Recalde, Plaza Moyúa, Gran Vía and Bilbao Intermodal. From the city centre, Bizkaia Aretoa can be reached by local public transport, taxi or on foot.

Check the official Bilbao Airport and Bizkaibus A3247 pages for current timetables and fares.

By train within Spain

Bilbao-Abando is the city’s main railway hub, with Renfe long-distance and regional services, including direct connections with Madrid. The station is at Plaza Circular in central Bilbao and connects with Metro Bilbao, local buses and commuter rail services.

Participants travelling from elsewhere in Spain can check routes and current schedules through Renfe.
