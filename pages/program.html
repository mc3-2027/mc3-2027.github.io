Scientific program

Scientific program

The 3MC3 scientific program will bring together keynote lectures, oral presentations, short oral presentations, and poster sessions on mineral carbonation for cement and concrete. The conference follows the MC3 single-room philosophy, without parallel sessions, to maximise discussion and exchange between participants.

At a glance

Program structure

Keynote lectures

Invited talks from international leaders in carbonation science and low-carbon materials.

Technical sessions

Oral contributions on mechanisms, recycling, mineralization, binders, and scale-up.

Poster sessions

Interactive presentation format for detailed studies, methods, and emerging results.

RILEM Technical Committee meetings

309-MCP: Accelerated mineral carbonation for the production of construction materials.

311-MBC: Magnesia-based binders in concrete.
