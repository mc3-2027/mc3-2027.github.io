Abstract submission

Submit your abstract

The 3rd International Conference on Mineral Carbonation for Cement and Concrete (MC3 Bilbao 2027) invites researchers, scientists, engineers, and industry professionals to submit original scientific contributions. Abstracts must be submitted through the official online form and may be considered for oral or poster presentations.

Open submission form

Submission Procedure

Complete the official online submission form with the following information:

  • Corresponding author: First name, last name, email address, institution, and country.
  • Authors and affiliations: Full author list in publication order, including the corresponding author, with numbered affiliations.
  • Abstract details: Title, scientific topic, and abstract body.
  • Presentation preferences: Oral presentation, poster presentation, or no preference, and the full name of the presenting author, who must appear in the author list.
  • Author declaration: Confirmation that all listed authors have approved the abstract content and agreed to its submission.
  • Data protection and privacy: The corresponding author must read the data protection information provided in the submission form and select “I agree” to proceed with a valid submission. Abstracts submitted with “I don't agree” cannot be accepted or processed.

Abstract Preparation

Language: All abstracts must be written in English.

Submission format: Abstracts must be pasted directly into the Microsoft Forms submission portal. No Word or PDF file is required.

Abstract length: Maximum 4,000 characters, including spaces. The limit applies to the abstract body, including any bibliographic references.

Separate fields: The abstract title, authors, affiliations, and keywords must be entered in separate fields of the submission form and are not included in the word or character limits. Do not include them again in the abstract.

Content: The abstract should clearly describe the research objectives, methodology, main results, and conclusions.

References: Bibliographic references may be included at the end of the abstract and count towards both length limits.

Figures and tables: Figures and tables are not accepted.

Important: Microsoft Forms may automatically truncate text exceeding 4,000 characters without displaying a warning. Authors are strongly advised to check that their abstract is complete before submission.

Review and Acceptance

All submitted abstracts will undergo scientific evaluation based on the following criteria:

  • Scientific quality
  • Originality
  • Relevance to the conference topics
  • Clarity of presentation

The Scientific Committee will make the final decision regarding abstract acceptance and presentation format, taking into account the scientific evaluation and programme availability.

Authors will be notified of the decision by 30 April 2027.

The notification email will include the final decision and the reviewers' comments approved for communication to the authors.

The corresponding author will receive all communications regarding the abstract submission and scientific review process.

Following a valid submission, a confirmation email containing a unique Abstract ID will be sent to the corresponding author's email address.

Authors should retain this identifier for future reference.

Publication opportunity

Special Issue in Cement and Concrete Research

Selected original contributions presented at 3MC3 Bilbao 2027 will be invited to submit a full paper to a Special Issue of Cement and Concrete Research dedicated to CO₂ mineralization for cement and concrete.

Invitation to the Special Issue will be reserved for original research contributions. Submitted manuscripts will undergo the journal’s standard peer-review process, and publication cannot be guaranteed.

The Special Issue will provide increased visibility for research presented at the conference. Accepted papers will be published open access, with no publication fees for authors.
